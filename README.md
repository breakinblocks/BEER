# Bookshelf Extended Enchanting Range (BEER)

Are you tired of the age-old struggle of cramming all your bookshelves within a measly two blocks of your enchanting table? Does your grand library feel wasted, its shelves ignored by the arcane arts?

Well, now no more! With Bookshelf Extended Enchanting Range (BEER), you can finally unleash the full potential of your enchanting room designs. BEER expands the valid range for bookshelves to a massive 16 blocks in any direction—giving you the freedom to build sprawling libraries, hidden alcoves, or towering archives, all while powering up your enchanting table like never before.

No more cramped corners. No more wasted space. Just pure enchanting power—cheers to that!

Install BEER today and raise a glass to enchanting without limits!
